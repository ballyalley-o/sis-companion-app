# Equipment sign-out and return

## Sign out a unit

1. Open Equipment and select the correct unit using its equipment code, name, photo, and serial details.
2. Confirm it is available, then select Sign out.
3. Choose the receiving driller and rig, and set the expected return period.
4. Record condition and notes; attach clear condition photos where needed.
5. Have the collector draw their signature before confirming.
6. Review the current loan and due date after saving.

The collector signature is required at sign-out. Record the actual recipient; do not sign on someone else's behalf.

## Return a unit

1. Open the unit's current loan and select Sign in.
2. Check the unit and its accessories.
3. Record the return condition, notes, and photos.
4. Confirm the return and check the resulting status.

Damaged returns go to maintenance. Good or fair returns become available under the current flow. Follow your warehouse's inspection process before issuing a unit again.

## Overdue equipment

Review overdue equipment and contact its recorded holder. Confirm the current rig or last known location and arrange return. An overdue indicator does not track a unit's physical position.

## Equipment details and photos

Administrators can edit the equipment name and serial and add or replace a photo. Authorised warehouse staff can add a missing photo within their warehouse. Capture or upload a clear image of the correct unit.

Loan history retains collection and return records. Older loans may show no signature if they were created before signature capture was implemented.
