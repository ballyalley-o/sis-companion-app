# Request or return stock

## Before you begin

Confirm the warehouse, driller, rig, and item identity. A stock return records stock coming back; it is separate from equipment loan return.

## Submit a request

1. Open More, then New stock request.
2. Choose Take stock or Return stock.
3. Choose the warehouse, driller, and rig.
4. Search for each item and choose its bin. For an item without a stock location, choose the requested receiving bin.
5. Set the quantity and add useful notes such as a job or hole reference.
6. Submit, then open the request to track its status.

## When the bin is short

The quantity control lets you request the full amount required. A warning identifies a shortage in the selected bin. Warehouse staff review all bins before deciding whether to purchase.

A submitted request does not reserve stock. Approval still checks the actual stock in the selected bins.

## Follow your request

Open More, then Stock requests. Review the status and audit trail. Awaiting purchase means the warehouse is arranging a shortage purchase; it does not mean the supplier has received an order.
