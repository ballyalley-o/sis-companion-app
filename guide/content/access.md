# Access and sign-in

## Request an account

1. Open the public invitation link or scan the invitation QR supplied by your administrator.
2. Enter your full name, work email, and home warehouse.
3. Submit the request and wait for an administrator to approve it.
4. Obtain your sign-in details privately from the administrator.
5. Open SIS Companion and sign in.

Submitting a form does not create an active account. Existing account emails do not appear as new pending requests.

## Administrator review

1. Open More, then Users & roles.
2. Review Pending access requests. Use Refresh if needed; the screen also refreshes periodically.
3. Select Review, set the temporary password, then approve and create the driller account.
4. Share the sign-in details privately. The current flow does not send an invitation email automatically.

Invite driller opens a shareable link and QR. It is an invitation to request access, not automatic account approval.

## Private deployment access

A hosting-provider access screen and the SIS sign-in screen are separate controls. Approval of an SIS account does not automatically give the person access through a protected hosting deployment. Contact the deployment administrator if you cannot reach the app sign-in screen.

## Google sign-in

Google sign-in depends on deployment configuration and an existing authorised SIS account. It is not an open registration route.
