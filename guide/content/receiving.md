# Receive a purchase order

For warehouse managers and administrators.

## Find the order

1. Open Orders or More, then Zoho purchase orders.
2. Enter or scan the purchase-order number or supplier reference.
3. Confirm the supplier, reference, and ordered items before counting.

## Count and put away

1. Focus the item-scanning field when using a USB or Bluetooth scanner that behaves like a keyboard.
2. Scan the item barcode or SKU. Each recognised scan adds one to its count.
3. Review or correct each item's count.
4. Select the put-away bin separately for every item.
5. Post the counted receipt to local stock and review the result.

The order screen identifies items needing a local SKU link or quantity review. Resolve those warnings before receiving them.

## Integration boundary

Purchase-order receiving records local SIS stock and history. It does not update Zoho inventory or the Zoho purchase order in the current implementation.

## Complete the related request

If the order replenishes a stock request, receive into its requested bins and return to that original request for approval. Receipt entry alone does not fulfil the driller's request.
