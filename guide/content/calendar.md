# Trips and calendar

## Plan a trip

1. Open More, then Trip calendar.
2. Select a date and choose the add control.
3. Enter the trip information, destination, and required stock.
4. Submit for the warehouse review process.

## Review the schedule

Use the month controls to move between dates and the status filters to narrow the calendar. Select a day to see its trips, then open a trip for details.

Warehouse staff review, prepare, and issue stock through the available trip actions. Check the saved status after each action; a calendar entry alone is not evidence that stock has been issued.

## Addresses

Use the address actions available on your deployment to open a map or directions. Address suggestions depend on the configured provider. Confirm the destination before travelling.

## Reorders

The Reorders screen also tracks reorder records associated with trips and low stock. These records are distinct from the stock-request purchase draft. Do not assume that marking a local reorder Ordered has sent a supplier PO in Zoho.
