# Welcome to SIS Companion

Your guide to requesting stock, finding a bin, receiving an order, and tracking equipment across the QLD and NSW warehouses.

- [Get access and sign in](../access/)

## Choose your role

| Role | Start with | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Driller | Access, stock requests, equipment | Submit accurate requests and confirm equipment collection. |
| Warehouse manager | Approvals, purchasing, receiving | Review requests and manage the assigned warehouse. |
| Administrator | User access, integrations, operations | Manage accounts, configuration, and warehouse-wide access. |

## Read the status before acting

Use the warehouse selector to confirm the correct site. Mock or demo labels identify test data. A recorded bin or equipment home location is not a live physical position.

## About these guides

These guides describe the implementation inspected in the repository on 19 November 2026. Your deployed app may run an earlier build. [Check the app version](../release/) when reporting a difference.

Engineering specifications remain separate from these user instructions. A roadmap feature is not necessarily available in the app.
