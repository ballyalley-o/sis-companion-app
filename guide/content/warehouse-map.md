# Find an item in the warehouse

## Search and locate

1. Open Warehouse map.
2. Confirm the warehouse and search by product name, SKU, or equipment code.
3. Select the result and, where applicable, the desired stock location.
4. Select the highlighted section or Show exact bin to open the detailed rack view.
5. Follow the radar marker to the selected bin and read its level and bay label.

The detailed rack view scrolls horizontally. On a phone, swipe within the rack rather than the whole page.

## Read a bin code

SEC2-L1-B4 means Section 2, Level 1, Bay 4. Confirm the label on the physical rack before collecting stock.

| Section | Area |
| --- | --- |
| SEC1 | Operations |
| SEC2 | Consumables |
| SEC3 | Consumables |
| SEC4 | Consumables |

SEC4's first two levels contain eight positions each; its upper two contain fourteen. Section diagrams are schematic rather than dimensioned navigation plans.

## Scan a QR label

Open Scan bin and scan the rack's QR. Review the bin identity and contents before starting any stock action.

## Location limitations

The map shows recorded locations, not indoor GPS. Equipment's home bin is its storage location; a signed-out unit may be with a rig. Check loan status and holder before assuming it is physically in the warehouse.
